    FAQ

    Questions

    Does AdBanao publish my ad?

    No. AdBanao prepares the ad and checks it. You approve it, then copy the text and post it yourself.

    What does the check cover?

    It compares the price on the ad with your product details, looks for claims that need proof, and spots common spelling mistakes.

    Can I change the text on the ad?

    Yes. Click the name, feature or price on the ad and type. Checks run again straight away.

    Does it work in Bangla?

    The whole page switches between English and Bangla. The spelling check covers English words only for now.
